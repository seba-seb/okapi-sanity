/ Visual editing without lock-in,
on a stack you control. /

01

Inline editing

Text, rich text, images and links are edited on the page, with undo and draft autosave.

02

Blocks and components

Insert, reorder and reuse sections. Change a component once and every instance follows.

~1 KB

of JS on pages without motion. The editor never ships in the public bundle.

04

AI chat

Changes by instruction, validated against the schema and permissions. Bring your own API key.
